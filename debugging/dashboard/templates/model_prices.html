Model prices across backends

Compare USD per 1 million text tokens. Input and output have separate prices. Model IDs and rates belong to the selected backend; the same model can cost differently on different sites.

Provider catalogs and official pricing pages are used where an exact model match is available. Fallbacks from LiteLLM's community registry are labeled Reference estimate; they may be out of date or unavailable to your account. Open the source before buying. Azure needs your deployment/region quote; local infrastructure costs are separate.

The estimate multiplies the displayed base rates by these token counts. It excludes long-context tier adjustments, cache effects, additional reasoning tokens, tool/search/media charges, tax, top-up fees, discounts, and contracts. This is not a monthly bill or spending limit. Retrieval time is not a reference price's review date.
